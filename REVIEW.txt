Findings requiring attention

1. HIGH: the mandatory Medium cooldown benchmark fails repeatedly.
   Command: ./codexion 5 3000 200 200 200 10 400 fifo
   All five uninstrumented runs burned out at 3000-3001 ms, after only one
   compile per coder. Three of these runs were performed sequentially,
   separately from the initial benchmark batch. The checklist explicitly
   requires this command to finish all ten compiles without burnout.
   One observed sequence of compile starts was:
     coder 1: 0; coder 2: 601; coder 3: 1201; coder 4: 1801; coder 5: 2402.
   Coder 1 then burned out at 3000, before its next turn.
   src/reservation.c:58-64 requires a coder to lead both dongle queues.
   An earlier waiter can therefore block an otherwise available dongle
   while waiting for its other dongle, forcing a serial chain of grants.
   src/start.c:54 creates coders in order with no common start barrier;
   thread arrival order can establish this chain.
   Instrumented copies sometimes passed because tracing changed scheduling.
   Their success does not cancel the uninstrumented failures. A correction
   must retain FIFO fairness and cooldown while avoiding this serial chain.

2. HIGH: an expired burnout deadline can be overwritten before detection.
   src/acquire.c:49-50 reserves a pair and starts compiling without checking
   the previous deadline. src/acquire.c:29 then replaces last_compile_start.
   If the coder acquires arbiter_mutex before the overdue monitor, the
   monitor loses the evidence of that missed deadline. The completion path
   can likewise retire a coder before the monitor checks an expired deadline.
   A focused harness initialized a coder 100 ms past its deadline with free
   dongles, then called acquire_pair: it returned success and logged a new
   compile. This models a delayed monitor; it is not an observed natural
   reproduction of that interleaving in the production benchmarks.
   Deadline checking and compile-start/completion decisions need consistent
   arbitration so an already expired deadline cannot disappear.

3. Norm compliance is not ready to certify.
   src/state.c:28 exceeds 80 columns with tab expansion. Comments inside
   functions also occur at src/state.c:28, src/thread.c:20, and
   src/heap_order.c:27. Check these and declaration/header alignment with
   Norminette. Norminette is not installed here; no official Norm pass is
   claimed. The checklist treats a Norm failure as disqualifying.

Codexion implementation review
Date: 2026-10-29

Implemented
- Per-coder completed compile count and shared completion stop decision.
- Repeated acquire, compile, release, debug, refactor lifecycle. The final
  compile skips debug and refactor.
- Separate deadline monitor. The arbiter mutex protects stop, compile counts,
  compile start times, and the ordering of state and burnout messages.
- One coder takes its only dongle and waits for the monitor to report burnout.
- FIFO requests carry an arbiter-assigned sequence for equal-millisecond order.
- Equal-deadline EDF requests favor alternating seats to allow independent
  pairs to compile concurrently; earlier deadlines still take precedence.
